Frequently Asked Questions

My progress disappeared. What happened?

Pixel Bloom stores all progress locally on your device. Progress may reset if you uninstall the app, restore your device, or if the persistent store becomes corrupted. The app will automatically recover and start fresh if it detects a corrupted store. We don't currently support cloud sync; this is planned for a future update.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

Pixel Bloom v1.0 is fully free with no advertisements and no in-app purchases. A future update may add optional advertising to support continued development; we will announce any change ahead of time.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All levels, music, and progress work offline. No internet connection is needed for any feature.

The level music isn't playing.

Make sure your device isn't on silent mode and the volume is up. You can also check the in-app audio settings via the menu icon on the title screen. The music is original, created with Suno AI.

How do I report a bug?

Please email harunorinoowari@gmail.com with:

  • Your device model (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro)
  • iOS version (Settings > General > About)
  • App version (long-press the version label on the title screen in TestFlight builds)
  • A description of what happened and what you expected
  • A screenshot if relevant

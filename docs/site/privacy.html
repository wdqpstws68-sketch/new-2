Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Pixel Bloom ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application on iPhone or iPad. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about our data practices.

Information We Collect

We collect the following limited information:

  • On-device progress data: Your level completion status, streak, and game preferences are stored locally on your device using SwiftData. This data does not leave your device.
  • Advertising identifiers (via Google AdMob): If you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency dialog, AdMob may access your device's advertising identifier to display personalized ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized to you.
  • Device information (via Google AdMob): AdMob may collect device-level information such as IP address, device model, OS version, and approximate geographic location to deliver and measure advertising.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: Apple may share anonymized crash reports with us if you have enabled "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings. We use this data only to fix bugs.

How We Use Information

  • To deliver core game functionality (progress saving, daily challenges).
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • To improve the app by addressing crashes and stability issues.

Third Parties

Pixel Bloom uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — provides advertising. Google's privacy practices are described at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

When you first launch the app, iOS will ask whether you want to allow Pixel Bloom to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. This permission controls whether AdMob can use your device's IDFA to personalize ads. You can change this setting any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Children's Privacy

Pixel Bloom is rated 4+ for general audiences but is not categorized as a "Made for Kids" app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

Data Retention

Progress data is retained on your device until you delete the app or reset your device. We do not store any of your gameplay information on our servers.

Your Choices

  • You can decline tracking via the App Tracking Transparency dialog.
  • You can opt out of AdMob personalized advertising in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • You can delete the app to remove all locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
